Simple process

How does it work?

1
See the challenge

The team opens the challenge in the Tangible Tournament App and works out what it is asking.

2
Put the device down

Learners build their solution together using the physical Tangible puzzle pieces.

3
Test the answer

Back to the app to run the code and see whether the car makes it round the track.

4
Change it and race again

If it does not work, change something and test again. That is where the learning happens.

A few things to know

Who can take part?

Schools, libraries, coding clubs and learning spaces outside South Africa can join directly. South African teams qualify through the Tangible National Championships.

Do I need to know how many teams I am bringing?

Not yet. Join now and register your final teams closer to the Coding Cup.

Do teachers need to know how to code?

No. Your role is to bring the learners together and let them work it out. We will help you get ready.

What if we do not have Tangible game packs?

Email support@levafoundation.org to purchase game packs before the tournament.

Start with one team

You do not need a computer lab, a coding background or a big budget. You need one team of learners willing to try things.
